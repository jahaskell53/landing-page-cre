# SMS opt-in - OpenMidmarket listing alerts

**Program:** OpenMidmarket listing-alert texts
**Sender:** OpenMidmarket (DBA), toll-free +1 844 409 3684
**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

This page is the public proof of consent for OpenMidmarket listing-alert SMS. It describes who the messages are for, how a user opts in, what we send, and how to opt out.

---

## Who we text

OpenMidmarket is a commercial real estate tool for brokers, property owners, and investors. Listing-alert texts go only to signed-in users who have explicitly turned on SMS for a saved search. We do not send these texts to people who have not opted in.

The program is not age-gated. It is not a lending program and does not send loan or credit offers.

---

## Message type

These are **transactional account / listing notifications**. When a new listing matches a saved search the user created, we may send a text about that match.

We do **not** send marketing blasts, promotional campaigns, or advertising SMS from this number.

---

## How users opt in (web form)

Opt-in happens in the signed-in web app. There is no SMS opt-in by texting a keyword, and we do not send listing-alert SMS until the user turns on the Text control.

1. Sign in at app.openmidmarket.com.
2. Open **Listing Alerts** at app.openmidmarket.com/analytics/listing-alerts.
3. Create and save a search.
4. Enable the **Text** / **Text alerts** switch (`notifySms`) for that saved search.

Only after that explicit toggle do we send SMS for that search. Creating a saved search alone, or turning on email alerts, does **not** opt the user into SMS.

The Listing Alerts table looks like this (the **Text** column is the opt-in control):

| Search | Email | Text | On |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downtown multifamily | On | **Off → On** | On |
| Industrial 50k+ SF | On | Off | On |

The **Text** column is a switch. It starts off. The user must turn it on. The **On** column is whether the saved search itself is active; it is separate from SMS consent.

---

## Sample message

Messages follow this shape:

> OpenMidmarket: New listing matched [search name]: [address]. [link] Reply STOP to opt out.

Example:

> OpenMidmarket: New listing matched Downtown multifamily: 100 Main St, Austin, TX. [listing link] Reply STOP to opt out.

## Phone number and frequency

Texts come from OpenMidmarket's toll-free number, **+1 844 409 3684**.

Frequency varies with how often matching listings appear. Typical volume is low: a user may receive no texts for a stretch, then a small number when new matches show up. We do not send a fixed daily or weekly blast.

Message and data rates may apply.

---

## How to opt out or get help

- Reply **STOP** to opt out of listing-alert texts. After STOP, we will not send further listing-alert SMS unless the user opts in again.
- Reply **HELP** for help.
- You can also turn off the **Text** / **Text alerts** switch for a saved search on the Listing Alerts page in the app.
