# OpenMidmarket Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 19, 2026
**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OpenMidmarket ("OpenMidmarket," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website at openmidmarket.com, our web application at app.openmidmarket.com, our newsletters, and related services (together, the "Services").

OpenMidmarket is a commercial real estate tool for brokers, property owners, and investors. It offers rent and sales comps, property valuation, rent surveys, market research, market news, and related features.

If you have questions, contact us at hello@openmidmarket.com. <!-- pragma: allowlist secret -->

---

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account information.** Your name, email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and profile details such as your photo, website, and role (for example, broker or owner).
- **Google sign-in.** If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us. We request only basic profile access (your email address and basic profile).
- **Waitlist and early-access information.** Your email address, role, the states and asset classes you work in, your deal volume, your goals, and how you heard about us.
- **Property and portfolio information.** Properties you add or analyze, such as addresses, unit mix, rents, occupancy, purchase price, NOI, loan terms, and ownership details, plus the comps, rent surveys, valuations, and saved searches you create.
- **Content you create.** Research chat prompts and conversations, files and images you upload (such as photos and PDFs), posts, comments, messages to other users, events you create, and event invitations and registrations.
- **Contacts you manage.** The names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, notes, and relationship details of people you add to your network or contacts features.
- **Newsletter preferences.** Your interests, regions, time zone, and preferred delivery times.
- **Communications.** Messages you send us, such as support requests and feedback.

### Information collected automatically

- **Usage and device data.** Pages viewed, features used, clicks, referring URLs, browser and device type, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and timestamps.
- **Session replay.** In our web app, we use PostHog to record how you interact with pages (for example, mouse movements, clicks, and page changes) to help us fix bugs and improve usability.
- **Error and performance data.** Error reports and diagnostic logs, which may include the page or action you were on.
- **Cookies and similar technologies.** We use cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences (such as theme), and run analytics. See Section 8.

### Information from other sources

Much of the market data in OpenMidmarket (listings, rents, sales comps, building details, and news) comes from public sources and third-party data providers. That data is about properties and markets, but it can include names or contact details of listing brokers or property owners as they appear in public listings or records.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Services, including comps, valuations, rent surveys, research, network features, events, and newsletters.
- Create and secure your account, including sign-in and password reset.
- Personalize content, such as newsletters tailored to your interests, regions, and properties.
- Run AI features you use (see Section 3).
- Send service emails (such as account, security, invite, and event emails) and, if you subscribe, newsletters. You can unsubscribe from newsletters at any time using the link in each email.
- Understand usage, debug problems, and improve the Services.
- Prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party targeted advertising.

## 3. AI features

Some features use large language models and other AI services. These include research chat, comp selection and narration, property photo grading, location ranking, newsletter writing, and news categorization. When you use them, the information needed for the task is sent to our AI providers to generate a response. That information may include your prompts, uploaded files, and property details.

We use the following AI providers: Anthropic, Google (Gemini API), and OpenAI. We also use Langfuse to log AI requests and responses so we can monitor quality and fix problems. Some research tasks may run code in an isolated sandbox hosted by E2B, or use Firecrawl to search and read public web pages.

We use these providers under their business/API terms. We do not use Google user data to train AI models (see Section 4).

AI output can be wrong. Check important figures before you rely on them.

## 4. Google user data

OpenMidmarket's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

- **Google sign-in:** we use your Google name, email address, and profile picture only to create and identify your account and display your profile.
- We **do not** use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
- We **do not** use Google user data to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.
- We transfer Google user data to others only as needed to provide or improve the features above (for example, to our hosting and database providers), to comply with law, for security purposes, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.
- People at OpenMidmarket do not read your Google user data unless you give us permission (for example, for a support request), it is needed for security or to comply with law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

You can revoke our access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## 7. Retention

We keep your information as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete your account and associated data from our production systems, except where we need to keep it for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons. Newsletter subscriber records are kept until you unsubscribe and ask us to delete them.

## 8. Cookies and analytics

We use:

- **Essential cookies and storage** to sign you in and keep your session secure.
- **Preference storage** to remember settings such as your theme.
- **Analytics** (PostHog) to understand usage and fix problems. In the web app, analytics runs only in production.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. If you block essential cookies, you may not be able to sign in. We do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a request to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing," although we do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law.
